# Changelog

## 1.2.5

When you browse your saves or catch up on what's new, it lists them for you: only a search and a suggestion show them as cards.

Where your app shows your saves as cards, it names them without links: tap a card to open it.

In Claude, connecting starts at mindlist.ai/connect/claude, which opens Claude's connect box with mindlist filled in.

## 1.2.4

When your app already shows your saves as cards, it doesn't list them again: it names the closest ones and says what's useful about them. It runs one search for a question about your saves.

## 1.2.3

When mindlist isn't signed in yet, it gives the step for where you are: in a terminal, type /mcp, choose mindlist, then Authenticate; in the Claude desktop app, type /mcp with nothing after it, open mindlist, then click Connect.

It checks your mindlist first when you start a piece of work, before another skill's questions, and also when you ask why something keeps going wrong.

## 1.2.2

When mindlist isn't signed in yet in Claude Code, it says how: type /mcp, choose mindlist, then Authenticate.

## 1.2.1

Suggestions now always follow your switch and your choices.

## 1.2.0

It checks at the start of a piece of work, including choosing a tool, how-to questions, learning and problems that keep coming back, and stays quiet on quick questions, the code at hand, pasted text and anything personal or hard. The offer fits what you need, and it says how many more saves fit. Tell it "not these", "more like this" or "I tried that" and mindlist keeps it, for your next chat and every app. A line in a project's instructions ties the project to one of your mindlists. It never remarks on whether you've read a save, and a search says which saves you told it you tried. Turn it off on mindlist.ai, Settings, AI tools.

## 1.0.0

The first version: the mindlist connector and the mindlist skill in one plugin, for Claude and Codex.
