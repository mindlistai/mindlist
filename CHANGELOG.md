## 1.2.0

It checks at the start of a piece of work, including choosing a tool, how-to questions, learning and problems that keep coming back, and stays quiet on quick questions, the code at hand, pasted text and anything personal or hard. The offer fits what you need, and it says how many more saves fit. Tell it "not these", "more like this" or "I tried that" and mindlist keeps it, for your next chat and every app. A line in a project's instructions ties the project to one of your mindlists. It never remarks on whether you've read a save, and a search says which saves you told it you tried. Turn it off on mindlist.ai, Settings, AI tools.

The first version: the mindlist connector and the mindlist skill in one plugin, for Claude and Codex.
