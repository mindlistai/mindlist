# mindlist

Your mindlist saves in Claude and Codex. One install does two things: it connects Claude or Codex to your saves through the mindlist connector, and it adds a skill that knows when your saves can help, finds them, and cites each one it uses.

## Use it

Ask "What did I save about pricing pages?", "Use my saves for this", "Turn my saves on onboarding into a brief", or "What's new in my mindlist?". In Claude, connect it once: Customize, Plugins, mindlist, Connectors, Connect, then sign in with the email code mindlist sends.

## While you work

Claude and Codex use your mindlist when it's relevant. When you start a piece of work (choosing a tool, planning, researching, writing, making something, learning, or a problem that keeps coming back), the skill can send your mindlist a few words about it, once per topic. When saves clearly fit, up to 3, it adds a short line for each, quoting your own note if you wrote one, says how many more there are, and offers what fits: to evaluate them, compare them, use them in your draft, or pull them all. If nothing clearly fits, you see nothing. It stays quiet on quick questions, the code at hand, text you pasted, and anything personal or hard.

Tell it any time "not these", "more like this" or "I tried that": mindlist keeps that choice, for a save, a site or a tag, and it holds in your next chat and in every app, even on a Read only connection. See and undo every choice, or turn suggestions off, on mindlist.ai, Settings, AI tools.

## What it reads and never writes

It reads your saves through your own connection: titles, summaries, highlights, tags, your notes, and the full text of a save it uses. It never edits, deletes, tags or shares a save. It moves your "what's new" position after showing you the new saves, and it saves a link only when you name that link and ask. What Claude or Codex reads is handled under your own agreement with Anthropic or OpenAI.

## Data

The skill is text only and runs nothing on your computer. It sends nothing anywhere except your own mindlist connection at mindlist.ai, which passes a suggestion's few words to Google to search by meaning and doesn't store them. Each call it makes carries the mark via "skill", so mindlist can count how many people use the skill. mindlist keeps the choices you tell it to keep, which saves it brought back and whether you opened them (for 90 days), and daily counts, never words from your chats.
